FEATURE 2: USER PERSONA DETECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

After country selection, ask 3 short profiling questions:
  1. "What describes you best?" 
     Options: Student | Working Professional | Self-Employed | Senior Citizen | Unemployed | Other
  2. "Have you applied for this type of form before?"
     Options: First time | Applied before but it was rejected | Applied before and got it | Not sure
  3. "How comfortable are you with paperwork?"
     Options: I find it confusing | I manage okay | I'm comfortable with it

Use these answers to:
  - adjust question complexity (students and seniors get simpler phrasing),
  - add relevant tooltips (first-timers see "why we ask this" on each question),
  - pre-warn about commonly missing docs for their persona,
  - set the tone of the checklist (casual vs. formal).

Store persona as: { role, priorExperience, comfort }
Persona affects the ENTIRE downstream experience.

FEATURE 3: AI-POWERED QUESTION + ANSWER ENGINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use the Replit OpenAI integration (already set up) for three jobs:

JOB 1 — QUESTION SIMPLIFICATION
  For each form field, use the LLM to generate a plain-language question
  appropriate for the detected persona.
  
  Example:
    Field label: "Annual gross income from all sources"
    Persona: Senior citizen, confused with paperwork
    Generated question: "How much money do you receive each year in total — 
    including pension, savings interest, and any other income?"

JOB 2 — ANSWER INTERPRETATION
  Accept free-form natural language answers and extract structured values.
  
  Examples:
    User types: "about 2 lakhs a year"  →  extract: 200000, currency: INR, period: annual
    User types: "I live with my son in his house"  →  extract: occupancy: dependent family member
    User types: "been here 12 years"  →  extract: residency_years: 12

JOB 3 — INCONSISTENCY DETECTION
  After all answers are collected, run a consistency check prompt:
  
  Check for:
    - Claims unemployed but listed an employer name
    - Income seems inconsistent with stated occupation
    - Address fields do not match
    - Date of birth makes stated age impossible
    - Residency duration contradicts stated address history
  
  Output: list of flagged inconsistencies with plain-language explanation.

FALLBACK (if OpenAI is unavailable):
  Fall back to deterministic rules-based parsing.
  Do NOT block the user — silently degrade gracefully.
  Log that AI was unavailable and rules fallback was used.

On the preview screen, show a REJECTION RISK SCORE from 0–100.

Calculate it as:
  Base: 100 (perfect)
  -10 per missing required field
  -15 per flagged inconsistency
  -5 per optional field left blank
  -10 per common rejection reason triggered

Display as:
  - A colored gauge (green 0–30, amber 31–60, red 61–100)
  - A headline: "Low Risk — Your form looks complete"
    or "High Risk — 3 issues may cause rejection"
  - A clickable list of risk reasons

This must update in real time as the user edits their answers in the preview.
